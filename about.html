About

We make the games.

Gorilla Troop is a Gorilla Portfolio studio. Short-session casual games with readable boards and satisfying feedback. Players live at troop.games.

Purpose

Build the games. Send players to play.

The studio exists to design and ship casual games that fit a commute — placement puzzles, clear boards, juice without clutter.

Gorilla Troop is a DBA of Gorilla Tech Advisors. Live titles are SWIVEL and ORCHARD. The player site is troop.games.
