About

A studio for short sessions.

Gorilla Troop is a Gorilla Group studio. We make short-session casual games with readable boards and satisfying feedback. Players live at troop.games.

Purpose

Build the games. Send players to play.

The studio exists to design and ship casual games that fit a commute — placement puzzles, clear boards, juice without clutter.

Gorilla Troop is a DBA of Gorilla Tech Advisors. The player site is troop.games.
